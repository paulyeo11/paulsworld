What Actually Happened实际发生了什么

T29's Bang Trade Analysis tab had been mixing three different kinds of numbers for its "still open" positions: SMH and AVGO used a modeled theta-decay estimate pegged to a reference share price ($569 and $397), while SPY and IWM used a real Positions-tab snapshot — except IWM's snapshot had never actually been filled in with a live figure. None of these were wrong on purpose, but none of them were as good as just reading the real numbers off the latest IBKR statement. T29的「Bang交易分析」页签此前对「仍持仓」的仓位混用了三种不同的数字：SMH和AVGO使用锚定参考股价（$569与$397）的建模theta衰减估算；SPY和IWM则使用「持仓」页签的真实快照——只是IWM的快照其实从未真正填入过实时数字。这些做法都不是故意出错，但都比不上直接从最新IBKR报表读取真实数字。

The 27 Jul PortfolioAnalyst statement's Open Position Summary lists Unrealized P&L per option leg. Summing the 4 legs for each symbol — and cross-checking against the statement's own Options-section total (−$14.53 across all open legs, which matched exactly) — gives a verified, real number for each position: SMH +$42.42, AVGO +$63.48, SPY +$73.50, IWM +$3.48. These now replace the mixed modeled/stale figures throughout the SMH, AVGO, SPY, and IWM sub-tabs (Table 420/470/510/550 and their timelines) and the Table 900 Summary. 7月27日投资组合分析报表的「持仓摘要」列出了每条期权腿的未实现盈亏。将每个代码的4条腿相加——并与报表本身「期权」章节的合计数（所有持仓腿合计−$14.53，完全吻合）核对——即可得出每个持仓经过核实的真实数字：SMH +$42.42、AVGO +$63.48、SPY +$73.50、IWM +$3.48。这些数字已取代SMH、AVGO、SPY、IWM各页签（表420/470/510/550及其时间线）与表900总览中原先混用的建模/过期数字。
