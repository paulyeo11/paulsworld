What Actually Happened实际发生了什么

Phase 2 (the 24 Jul re-open, 2 lots at the same 680P/675P/745C/750C strikes, 28 Aug expiry) collected $470 in premium up front. By the time it closed, QQQ had moved enough that buying back the short legs and selling out of the long legs cost a net $451 — leaving only $10.53 of that $470 as real profit, once actual commissions are counted. The 27 Jul statement's per-leg Realized P&L confirms it: 745C +$910.89, 750C −$775.07, 675P +$822.86, 680P −$948.15, netting to +$10.53 for the whole 2-lot condor. 第二阶段（7月24日重新建仓，2口，行权价仍为680P/675P/745C/750C，8月28日到期）建仓时收取了$470权利金。到平仓时，QQQ的走势已使买回空头腿、卖出多头腿的净成本达到$451——计入实际佣金后，$470权利金中只剩下$10.53的真实利润。7月27日报表逐条腿的已实现盈亏也印证了这一点：745C +$910.89、750C −$775.07、675P +$822.86、680P −$948.15，整个2口铁鹰净盈亏为+$10.53。

This doesn't change Phase 1's result — that trade (1 lot, 17→23 Jul) was already closed and banked +$32 well before this update, using real fill confirmations at the time.
