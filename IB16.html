Why "Price Never Crossed the Strike" Still Lost Money为什么「股价从未突破行权价」仍会亏损

An Iron Condor's P&L on close isn't a simple "did price breach the strike" test — it's Net Credit Received at open minus Debit Paid to close. An option's price is driven by how close the stock is to that strike (and how much time is left), not just whether it's technically in-the-money or out-of-the-money. SPY rallied hard toward the 770 short call strike this cycle. Even staying below it, that proximity pushed the 770 call's price from $4.47 at entry up to $11.29 to buy back — a much bigger move than the small decay/offset the 780 long call provided. 铁鹰组合平仓时的盈亏，并非简单的「股价是否突破行权价」测试——而是建仓时收取的净权利金 减去 平仓时支付的净成本。期权价格由股价与该行权价的距离（以及剩余时间）决定，而非仅仅取决于是否技术性价内/价外。本轮SPY股价强势上涨，逼近770卖出认购行权价。即便始终未突破，这种「逼近」已将770认购的价格从建仓时的$4.47推高至买回时的$11.29——远大于780买入认购所能提供的抵消/衰减幅度。
