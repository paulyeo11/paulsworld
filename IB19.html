The One Lesson: Distance, Not Just Direction最大的教训：距离，而非方向

SPY is the standout, and not because price ever breached a strike — it never did. SPY rallied hard toward the 770 short call, and even without crossing it, the option's price jumped from proximity and IV alone: bought back at $11.29 versus sold at $4.47. The put side's gain (+$90) wasn't close to enough to offset the call side's loss (−$231), for a net −$148.60. The takeaway: an Iron Condor's P&L moves with how close price gets to a short strike, not just whether it actually crosses it — a lesson worth remembering the next time a condor's short strike comes under pressure before expiry. SPY是本月最突出的一笔，而且不是因为价格真的穿越了执行价——它始终没有。SPY强力上涨逼近770的卖出看涨执行价，即使没有真正穿越，期权价格也会因为距离与隐含波动率而跳升：买回价$11.29，对比卖出价$4.47。认沽一侧的获利（+$90）远不足以抵消认购一侧的亏损（−$231），净亏损−$148.60。启示：铁鹰的盈亏取决于价格与卖出执行价的距离，而不只是是否真正穿越——下次铁鹰的卖出执行价在到期前承压时，值得记住这一点。
