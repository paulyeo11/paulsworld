⚠️ Share Price (B), Price Swing % (D), and Variance % (C) use daily closing prices fetched once per page load (Stooq/Yahoo daily feed) — "Last Week/Month/Quarter/2 Qtr Ago/3 Qtr Ago/4 Qtr Ago" are approximate trading-day lookbacks (≈5 / 21 / 63 / 126 / 189 / 252 trading days ago), not exact calendar dates. Price Swing (D) is the high-low swing from (B)'s point through today: F = the highest close in that window, G = the lowest, D = (F−G)/F (e.g. high $100 / low $80 → 20%). Variance (C) is different — it compares today's price (A) to the selected period's price (B) directly. If a company doesn't have enough price history for a lookback, (B), (C), and (D) show "—" rather than a guessed value.股价（B）、股价波动（D）与涨跌幅（C）使用每次载入页面时抓取一次的每日收盘价（Stooq／Yahoo每日数据源）——「上週／上月／上季／2季度前／3季度前／4季度前」为约略交易日回溯（约5／21／63／126／189／252个交易日前），并非精确日历日期。股价波动（D）计算从（B）的时间点到今天之间的最高最低价波动：F为该区间内最高收盘价，G为最低收盘价，D=(F−G)/F（例如最高$100／最低$80 → 20%）。涨跌幅（C）不同——直接比较今天股价（A）与所选期间的股价（B）。若某公司历史价格数据不足以计算该期间，（B）、（C）与（D）会显示「—」，不会用猜测值填补。
