What Actually Happened实际发生了什么

The NAV walk tells the story cleanly: Deposits, Dividends and Interest were all unchanged from the last statement (no new cash moved), Fees ticked up slightly (−$130.14 vs −$126.12), and the Market Value Change line improved from −$5,439.18 to −$3,287.95 — a $2,151.23 swing in Paul's favor. Nearly all of that came from the stock side: FDS, POOL and ZTS (the account's only open stock positions) collectively narrowed their unrealized loss from −$22,981.15 to −$20,370.15, a $2,611.00 improvement, as all three names recovered somewhat. The Allocation tab's Long ($64,607.58) minus Short (−$6,630.26) also ties out exactly to the new $57,977.32 Ending NAV, confirming the number independently of the NAV walk. 净值走势清楚说明了原因：存款、股息、利息与上次报表相比均无变化（没有新的资金进出），手续费小幅上升（−$130.14，此前为−$126.12），而市值变动一行则从−$5,439.18改善至−$3,287.95——对Paul有利的摆动达$2,151.23。这部分几乎全部来自股票一侧：FDS、POOL、ZTS（账户仅有的持仓股票）合计未实现亏损从−$22,981.15收窄至−$20,370.15，改善了$2,611.00，三只股票均有所回升。「资产配置」页签的多头（$64,607.58）减去空头（−$6,630.26），也与新的期末净值$57,977.32完全吻合，独立核实了这一数字。

The four still-open Iron Condors moved in the opposite direction, but far more modestly. SMH's real unrealized P&L fell from +$42.42 to +$11.89, and AVGO's fell from +$63.48 to +$13.81 — both normal single-day mark-to-market swings on positions that still have real gamma exposure (24 and 31 DTE remaining respectively), not a modeling error. SPY (+$73.96, essentially flat vs +$73.50) and IWM (+$6.80, up from +$3.48) barely moved. Combined across all four, the option side's total unrealized P&L went from −$14.53 to −$407.99 — almost all of that swing is the FDS Covered Call, not the four condors, which stayed a small net positive together. 四个仍持仓的铁鹰走势则相反，但幅度小得多。SMH的真实未实现盈亏从+$42.42降至+$11.89，AVGO从+$63.48降至+$13.81——两者都是仍有真实gamma风险敞口的持仓（分别还剩24天与31天到期）常见的单日市值波动，并非建模出错。SPY（+$73.96，较+$73.50基本持平）与IWM（+$6.80，较+$3.48上升）则几乎没有变化。四者合计，期权一侧的未实现盈亏总额从−$14.53变为−$407.99——这一变动几乎全部来自FDS备兑看涨，而非四个铁鹰本身，后者合计仍为小幅净盈利。
