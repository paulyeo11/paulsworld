AI223: From Charts to Conversations — How AI Changed Data Visualization

AI223：从图表到对话——AI如何改变了数据可视化

We used to build the table first, then the chart, then hope the answer was hiding somewhere in it. Now I just tell AI my problem and hand it the raw data — it finds the answer for me.

以前，我们得先做表格，再做图表，然后祈祷答案就藏在里面。现在我只需要告诉AI我的问题，把原始数据交给它——它会帮我找出答案。

✏️ Paul Yeo 📅 29 Jul 2026 ⏰ 4 min read
✅ Link copied!
For years, "data visualization" meant a spreadsheet, a pivot table, and a chart wizard — and it was on me to know which chart type, which columns, and what the numbers actually meant. Today the job has flipped: I describe my problem in plain words, hand over the raw data, and AI works out what to show me and why. 多年来，“数据可视化”就是一张电子表格、一个数据透视表，加上图表向导——而选哪种图表、用哪些栏位、数字到底代表什么，全都得靠我自己搞懂。今天，这份工作反过来了：我只需要用大白话描述我的问题，把原始数据交出去，AI会自己想清楚该给我看什么、为什么这样看。

📊 The Old Way: Build the Table, Then Hope📊 旧方法：先做表格，再靠运气

Growing up in spreadsheets, solving a problem with data meant a very specific chain of manual steps: pull the numbers into a table, decide which columns to compare, pick a chart type (bar? line? pie?), format the axes, and only then squint at the result to see if it actually answered my question. If it didn't, I'd rebuild the table a different way and try again.

在电子表格里长大的做法是这样的：把数字拉进一个表格，决定要比较哪些栏位，挑一种图表类型（柱状图？折线图？饼图？），设置好坐标轴，然后才眯着眼看结果，看它到底有没有回答我的问题。如果没有，就换一种方式重做表格，再试一次。

The tool never knew what I was actually trying to figure out. I had to translate my real question — "is my Iron Condor cushion getting tight?" — into a chart-building task myself, every single time.

工具从来不知道我真正想弄清楚的是什么。每一次，我都得自己把真正的问题——“我的铁鹰策略安全垫是不是变紧了？”——翻译成一个“做图表”的任务。

· · ·

🤖 The New Way: Ask, Don't Build🤖 新方法：直接问，不用自己动手做

📊 Before — I Build the Chart📊 以前——我自己做图表

I decide the chart type, the columns, the colours, the axis scale — before I even know if that chart will answer my question. The thinking happens after the chart exists.

我得先决定图表类型、栏位、颜色、坐标轴刻度——而这时我根本还不知道这张图能不能回答我的问题。思考是在图表做出来之后才开始的。

🤖 Now — I Ask the Question🤖 现在——我直接问问题

I say what I actually want to know and hand over the raw data. AI decides what needs comparing, builds the table or chart that fits the question, and explains what it means. The thinking happens first.

我直接说出我真正想知道的事，把原始数据交出去。AI会自己判断该比较什么，做出符合问题的表格或图表，再解释这代表什么意思。思考是先发生的。

The old way asked "what chart should I make?" The new way asks "what's my problem?" — and the chart is just AI's answer to that. 旧方法问的是“我该做什么图表？”新方法问的是“我的问题是什么？”——图表只是AI给出的答案。
· · ·

🔍 What This Actually Looks Like For Me🔍 这在我身上具体是怎么发生的

  • Example 1例子一
    IBKR statements. Instead of building a NAV chart column by column, I just say "update my IBKR data" and hand over the raw PortfolioAnalyst export. AI cross-checks the numbers across sections, builds the chart on T29, and tells me in plain words what actually changed — Ending NAV, unrealized P&L, deposits. IBKR对账单。我不用一栏一栏地做NAV图表，只需要说“更新我的IBKR数据”，把原始的PortfolioAnalyst导出档交出去。AI会跨栏位核对数字，在T29上做出图表，然后用大白话告诉我到底发生了什么变化——期末净值、未实现盈亏、存款。
  • Example 2例子二
    Iron Condor cushion. My real question is "is any position getting risky?" — not "draw me a bar chart of share prices." AI reads the raw strikes and prices, flags which ones are tight, and only then shows the table that proves it — with the formula spelled out (A, B, C letters) so I can check its work. 铁鹰策略安全垫。我真正的问题是“有没有仓位正在变得危险？”——而不是“帮我画一张股价柱状图”。AI会读取原始的履约价和股价，标出哪些已经变紧，然后才展示证明这一点的表格——还会写清楚公式（用A、B、C字母标示），让我自己也能核对它的算法。
  • Example 3例子三
    No dashboard-building skill required. I never had to learn how to make a pivot table or pick a chart library. I just describe the problem in the same plain language I'd use talking to a person, and the visualization comes out the other end already built. 不需要学会做仪表板的技能。我从来不需要学怎么做数据透视表，或挑图表库。我只需要用平常和人说话一样的大白话描述问题，可视化的结果就已经自动做好了。
· · ·

✅ Why This Matters to Me✅ 这对我来说为什么重要

I'm 70 and retired — I never studied data analytics, and I was never going to become a spreadsheet wizard at this stage of life. The old way of doing data visualization put the burden of "which chart, which columns" entirely on me, the person who understood the problem least about chart-building software. The new way flips that: I bring the problem and the raw numbers, AI brings the chart-building skill.

我今年70岁，退休了——我从来没学过数据分析，这个人生阶段也不打算成为电子表格高手。旧的数据可视化方式，把“该用哪种图表、哪些栏位”的负担全压在我身上——而我恰恰是最不懂做图表软件的那个人。新方法把这一切反过来了：我带来问题和原始数字，AI带来做图表的本事。

That's the real shift. It's not that the charts look prettier now — it's that I no longer have to be the one who knows how to build them.

这才是真正的转变。不是图表变得更漂亮了——而是我再也不需要是那个懂得怎么做图表的人。

  • 让AI自己选出真正能回答问题的表格/图表类型，而不是我瞎猜的那种
