📸 Recommended Photo Stop📸 推荐拍照点
B. Kelamei Service Area (克拉美丽服务区)B. 克拉美丽服务区
Xinjiang's first desert-park-themed highway service area, opened 2024 — right in the Gurbantunggut Desert dunes, ~180 km / ~2 hr from your Urumqi hotel. Camel rides, desert dune buggies, and a hot-air-balloon viewpoint make it the natural "we're really in the desert now" photo break on this drive, not just a fuel stop. 新疆首个以"沙漠公园"为主题的高速服务区，2024年开园——就坐落在古尔班通古特沙漠的沙丘之中，距乌鲁木齐酒店约180公里/约2小时车程。骆驼骑行、沙漠越野车、热气球观景台，让这里成为此行"真正进入沙漠"的天然拍照点，而不只是加油休息站。

🍽️ Recommended Restaurant Stop🍽️ 推荐餐厅点
C. Wulungu Lake Golden Coast (乌伦古湖黄金海岸)C. 乌伦古湖黄金海岸
Time this one for sunset (~20:00 local clock time in late Aug, since Xinjiang runs on Beijing time) — a silver-sand "Gobi Sea" coastline with gulls and swans over the water. Fuhai's lakeside restaurants nearby serve the local specialty: spicy whole lake fish and deep-fried "five-spot black" fish — the Fuhai fish feast already on today's food plan. Eat here before the short final drive into Fuhai town. 建议在日落时分抵达（新疆用北京时间，8月底当地约晚上20:00）——银色沙滩绵延的"戈壁海"海岸线，海鸥天鹅栖息湖面。附近福海湖畔餐厅供应当地特色：辣味全鱼宴与油炸"五道黑"——正是今日美食计划中的福海鱼宴。用餐后再短途驱车前往福海县城。
