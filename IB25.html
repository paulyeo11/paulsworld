Strike Sits Below Cost Basis行权价低于成本基础

POOL's avg cost is $278.40 — the $230 strike sold tonight sits $48.40 below that. If POOL rallies past $230 by the Sep 18'26 expiry and the call gets assigned, the shares get called away at $230, realizing a loss vs the $278.40 cost basis, only partly cushioned by the $269 premium collected (net loss if assigned ≈ (230−278.40)×100 + 269 = −$4,571). This is an income trade while the stock is still well underwater from its cost basis, not an exit strategy — the same "premium collected but strike below cost" pattern already logged for POOL's earlier covered calls (e.g. the 17 Jul 260-strike call that expired worthless). Below $230, the shares and the premium both stay Paul's. POOL的平均成本为$278.40——今晚卖出的$230行权价低于成本$48.40。若POOL在2026年9月18日到期前上涨突破$230并被行权，股票将以$230被买走，相对$278.40成本基础实现亏损，仅由已收取的$269权利金部分抵消（若被行权，净亏损约为(230−278.40)×100+269=−$4,571）。这是在股价仍大幅低于成本基础时进行的收取权利金交易，而非退出策略——与POOL此前备兑看涨期权（如7月17日260行权价、到期价外归零的那笔）「收取权利金但行权价低于成本」的模式相同。若股价维持在$230以下，股票与权利金都将保留。
