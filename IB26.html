Why the Ratio Mattered为什么比例很重要

The first version of this trade (Sell 200P / Buy 190P, $10 wide) previewed at Max Loss $910 vs Max Return $85 — a 10.7:1 ratio, and an outlier next to Paul's actual Iron Condor history (SPY 1.8:1, IWM 1.4:1, SMH 0.9:1, NVDA2 1.0:1). Two things were driving it: the ticket was pricing off the Ask (the worst realistic fill), and the short strike was quite far OTM (12.6% cushion) for only a $10-wide spread, so the credit collected was a thin slice of the risk. Moving the short strike up to 210 (8.2% cushion) and widening to $15 collected meaningfully more premium per dollar of width, and working the order near Mid instead of the Ask captured $322.72 instead of the ~$85 the first ticket would have defaulted to. 该交易的第一版（卖出200P／买入190P，价差宽度$10）在订单预览中显示最大亏损$910、最大盈利$85——比例达10.7:1，相较Paul实际的铁鹰持仓历史（SPY 1.8:1、IWM 1.4:1、SMH 0.9:1、NVDA2 1.0:1）明显偏离。造成这一情况的原因有两个：订单单据以卖价（Ask，最不利的现实成交价）计价；且卖出行权价距现价较远（缓冲12.6%），但价差宽度只有$10，导致所收权利金相对所承担风险显得单薄。将卖出行权价上调至210（缓冲8.2%）并将宽度扩大至$15，在单位宽度上收取了更多权利金；同时以接近中间价而非卖价成交，实际收到$322.72，而非第一版单据默认的约$85。
