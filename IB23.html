Why AAPL, Why Now为何选择AAPL，为何是现在

AAPL was flagged but not entered on 31 Jul 2026, right after the stock sold off roughly 7%+ post-earnings to around $302.91 on a Services revenue miss and weaker margin guidance. At the time, the recommendation was: a single-leg cash-secured put if bullish on a bounce and comfortable owning shares (GOOGL-style), or a 4-leg Iron Condor if expecting AAPL to simply range now that earnings were out. Paul went with the single-leg put. That earnings event is now well behind — AAPL's next report isn't due until after this position's Sep 18'26 expiry, so there's no earnings-gap risk sitting inside the open window (unlike the still-open NVDA2 condor, which does carry that risk into its 26 Aug 2026 report). AAPL曾于2026年7月31日被标记但未建仓，当时股价因服务业务营收未达预期及利润率指引疲软，财报后下跌约7%以上至约$302.91。当时给出的建议是：若看好反弹且愿意持有股票，可选单腿现金担保认沽（GOOGL式做法）；若预期财报后AAPL转为区间震荡，则可选4腿铁鹰。Paul最终选择了单腿认沽。该财报事件如今已成过去——AAPL下一次财报将在此持仓2026年9月18日到期之后才公布，因此持仓窗口内不存在财报跳空风险（不同于仍持仓中的NVDA2铁鹰，其2026年8月26日财报仍落在窗口内）。
