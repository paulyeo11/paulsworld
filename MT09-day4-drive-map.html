📸 Recommended Photo Stop📸 推荐拍照点
B. Kanas Lake Scenic Area (喀纳斯湖景区)B. 喀纳斯湖景区
This short transfer day exists to get you to the gateway of Kanas — the lake itself is tomorrow's full dawn-viewpoint outing, but the entry road already gives the first glimpses of the alpine scenery ahead. A good spot to pause and take it in before checking in at Jiadengyu. 今天这段短途转场，正是为了抵达喀纳斯的门户——湖区本身留待明日清晨观景台的完整行程，但入区道路已能一睹前方高山湖泊风光的初印象。抵达贾登峪入住前，值得在此驻足片刻。

🍽️ Dining Note🍽️ 用餐提示
C. Jiadengyu — Limited OptionsC. 贾登峪 — 选择有限
Unlike the other legs of this trip, there isn't a standout restaurant recommendation for tonight — Jiadengyu sits inside the scenic-area campground with limited dining choices. Hotel dining is the practical option; save the food splurge for Hemu (yesterday) or Burqin (in two days). 与此行其他路段不同，今晚没有特别推荐的餐厅——贾登峪位于景区汽车营地内，用餐选择有限。建议以酒店餐厅为主；把美食的期待留给昨天的禾木，或两天后的布尔津。
