AC03: Connecting AI to My Company's ERP Database

🛠️ What I Did🛠️ 我做了什么

Our field tech company runs on an ERP system to manage sales orders, invoices, and customer records. I set up an MCP connection with an API key to let AI reach into that database directly. As a first test, I kept it simple — I asked AI to update and read our sales order data, meaning all of our invoices.

我们的field tech公司用一套ERP系统来管理销售订单、发票和客户记录。我用一个API密钥设置了MCP连接，让AI能够直接读取那个数据库。作为第一次测试，我保持简单——我请AI更新并读取我们的销售订单数据，也就是我们所有的发票。

· · ·

✅ Benefit 1 & 2 — Easy to Connect, Fast to Update✅ 好处一和二——连接容易，更新超快

  • Very easy to connect. I was able to link AI to the ERP database without much issue at all — no complicated setup, no wrestling with the system. 连接非常容易。我几乎没遇到什么问题就把AI连上了ERP数据库——不需要复杂的设置，也不用跟系统搏斗。
  • Raw data updates very fast. Pulling and refreshing the sales order / invoice data through AI was much quicker than I expected. 原始数据更新速度非常快。通过AI拉取和刷新销售订单/发票数据，比我预期的要快得多。
· · ·

📈 Benefit 3 — AI Data Visualization is a Different World📈 好处三——AI数据可视化是完全不同的世界

When I got to the reporting part, I realised something important: doing data visualization with AI is a lot different from the past, when I used QlikView and Power BI. With those tools, I had to build the report myself. With AI, it analyses my data and gives me feedback immediately — so I can take action based on that feedback right away.

到了做报表这一步时，我意识到一件重要的事：用AI做数据可视化，和过去用QlikView、Power BI的时候非常不一样。用那些工具，报表得靠我自己去做。用AI，它会分析我的数据，立刻给我反馈——这样我就能马上根据反馈采取行动。

The old tools gave me a chart. AI gives me an answer — and tells me what to do about it. 旧工具给我的是一张图表。AI给我的是一个答案——还会告诉我该怎么处理。

👑 Example: Finding My Important Customers (80/20 Rule)👑 例子：找出我的重要客户（80/20法则）

I can simply ask AI to list out which are my important customers. In business, we need to focus on our important customers — the 80/20 rule. We focus 80% of our time on the 20% of customers who matter most, and that's how we achieve maximum results.

我可以直接请AI列出哪些是我的重要客户。做生意，我们需要聚焦在重要客户身上——也就是80/20法则。我们把80%的时间，投入在最重要的那20%客户身上，这样才能取得最大的成果。

💰 Example: Pinpointing Profit Problems💰 例子：精准找出利润问题

AI can also help me analyse gross profit or net profit, so that from the report I'm able to straightaway pinpoint which customers have low profit margins, why the margin is so low, and how to prevent this from happening — so the company can achieve maximum profit.

AI也能帮我分析毛利或净利，让我从报表中立刻精准地找出哪些客户的利润率偏低、为什么这么低，以及该如何预防这种情况再次发生——从而让公司获得最大利润。

There are many, many more reports I can ask AI to provide — and it is very easy to use.

还有非常非常多的报表可以请AI来提供——而且使用起来非常简单。

· · ·

💬 As Easy as Just Talking💬 就像聊天一样简单

What I need to do is simply talk to AI. Like right now, writing this very article — I just told AI what happened, and it took about five minutes to generate the article for me, so I can keep it as my achievement.

我需要做的，就是直接跟AI说话。就像现在写这篇文章一样——我只是告诉AI发生了什么事，它大约花了五分钟就帮我把文章写出来了，让我可以把这个记录下来，作为我的成就。
