Why This Was a Good Time to Close为什么此时是平仓的好时机

GOOGL's short put was sold to open on 29 Jul for $4.43/share ($443 gross, $441.86 net of commission). By tonight, the same option had decayed to around $1.10–1.21 as the stock moved well clear of the $305 strike — that decay is exactly what a short put is designed to capture. Buying it back locks in the difference: $441.86 collected minus $110.84 to close (the $110 buy-back plus $0.84 commission) equals +$331.02. GOOGL的这笔卖出认沽期权于7月29日以$4.43/股开仓（毛权利金$443，扣除手续费后净额$441.86）。到今晚，随着股价持续远离$305行权价，同一份期权的价值已衰减至约$1.10–1.21——这正是卖出认沽期权所要捕捉的时间价值衰减。买回平仓即锁定这一差额：已收$441.86，减去平仓成本$110.84（买回价$110加手续费$0.84），等于+$331.02。

The close-target rule that already governs Paul's 4-leg Iron Condors (buy back around 50% of credit collected, or 21 DTE, whichever comes first) applies just as well to a single-leg put — arguably more so here, since a naked short put carries unlimited downside risk down to zero if the stock craters, with no protective long leg underneath. At 74.7% captured with 36 days of remaining exposure for only ~25% more potential profit, the risk/reward of holding no longer favored staying in. 已适用于Paul4腿铁鹰组合的平仓目标法则（收取权利金约50%或剩21天到期，以先到者为准）同样适用于单腿认沽期权——甚至更适用，因为裸卖认沽在股价暴跌时下行风险理论上可至归零，且并无保护性买入腿托底。在已锁定74.7%权利金、仍剩36天风险敞口、却只剩约25%额外潜在获利空间的情况下，继续持有的风险回报比已不再划算。
