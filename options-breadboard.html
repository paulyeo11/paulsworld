🧮 Q: NVDA drops to $0 — max loss if I close the next day?问：若NVDA跌至$0——次日平仓最大亏损是多少？▸

  • Answer: −$285 — the same as the stated Max Loss above. A crash to $0 doesn't create extra risk beyond that cap; it just gets realized almost immediately instead of waiting for expiry.答案：−$285——与上面标示的最大亏损相同。跌至$0不会带来超出该上限的额外风险，只是提前（而非等到到期日）就把亏损兑现了。
  • Put side (195/190): at $0 both puts are deep ITM (delta ≈ 1, ~zero time value left), so the spread is already worth its full $5.00 width (−$500) even with 35 DTE remaining — no cushion from time left on the clock.认沽腿（195/190）：股价为$0时两腿均深度价内（delta≈1，几乎无时间价值），价差已达到其最大宽度$5.00（−$500）——即使还剩35天到期，也没有时间价值缓冲。
  • Call side (230/235): at $0 both calls are worthless — nothing owed to close that side.认购腿（230/235）：股价为$0时两腿均一文不值——平仓这一边无需支付。
  • Cost to close ≈ $500, minus the $215 credit received = −$285. Same number whether you close the next day or hold to expiry.平仓成本约$500，减去已收取的$215权利金 = −$285。无论是次日平仓还是持有至到期，数字相同。
If instead "drop zero" meant NVDA closes flat (no price change) today: no loss at all — you'd just be sitting on the mark-to-market credit near your $215 entry, minus a night of theta decay. 若「跌零」是指NVDA今日持平（价格无变化）：则完全没有亏损——只是持仓浮动盈亏仍接近入场时的$215权利金，扣除一晚的时间价值衰减（theta）。

🧮 Q: How is the Net Price $5.05 DEBIT computed from the Price column?问：净价$5.05借记是如何从「平仓价」栏计算出来的？▸

  • Short answer: it isn't fully computable from this table's Price column — unlike NVDA's Table 11. Only 2 of the 4 Price cells are filled in (680P $23.44, 745C $0.40); the other two (675P, 750C) are blank (—).简短答案：与表11的NVDA不同，本表的净价无法完全从「平仓价」栏推算出来。4格「平仓价」中只有2格有数据（680P $23.44、745C $0.40），另外2格（675P、750C）留空（—）。
  • Why: the shared 29 Jul closing alert closed a $10-wide combo (short 680P/745C, long 670P/755C), while this condor's actual wings are $5-wide (long 675P/750C). The short legs (680P, 745C) match exactly, so those two Price cells are real fills. The long legs don't match, so their close prices for this specific $5-wide combo are unknown — left blank rather than guessed.原因：7月29日分享的平仓提醒平的是$10宽组合（卖680P/745C，买670P/755C），而本铁鹰实际翼宽为$5（买675P/750C）。卖出腿（680P、745C）完全吻合，所以这两格是真实成交价；买入腿不吻合，因此本组$5宽价差的平仓价未知，留空而非猜测。
  • The $5.05 Net Price is therefore copied straight from the alert as the whole-combo close cost for the $10-wide version — a reference/approximate figure for this position, not a sum of these four Price cells.因此净价$5.05是直接从提醒中取用的整组$10宽版本平仓成本——是本仓位的参考/近似数字，并非这四格「平仓价」相加所得。
  • To get an exact, table-derived Net Price like NVDA's $2.47 (buy-to-close short legs minus sell-to-close long legs, added across both spreads), Paul would need the actual 675P and 750C closing fills for this specific $5-wide condor.若要像NVDA的$2.47那样，从表中数据精确算出净价（买回空头腿的成本，减去卖出多头腿的所得，两侧价差相加），需要Paul提供本组$5宽铁鹰675P与750C的实际平仓成交价。

❓ Q: Why is 11% cushion considered Safe but 4% is considered Tight (not safe)?问：为什么11%的缓冲算「安全」，而4%却算「偏紧」（不安全）？▸

  • The 5% line isn't arbitrary math — it's a rule-of-thumb buffer for "how much room is left before a single bad trading day matters."5%这条界线并非随意订的算式——它是一个经验法则缓冲值，衡量「在一个波动剧烈的交易日发生之前，还剩多少空间」。
  • 11% cushion = the stock would need to drop over a tenth of its value just to reach your short put strike. That's well beyond a normal day's move — even a rough day (2–3% down) leaves you nowhere near the strike. Plenty of room to react before it becomes urgent.11%缓冲 = 股价需要下跌超过十分之一才会碰到你卖出的认沽行权价。这远超一般单日波动幅度——即使是较差的一天（下跌2–3%），也离行权价还很远。有充足时间可以应对，不急迫。
  • 4% cushion = the stock is already close enough that one unusually volatile day — a bad macro headline, a semiconductor selloff, an earnings scare in a related name — could realistically erase that gap and put price right at (or through) your strike.   • But once priced correctly (near mid), Max Loss > Max Return is completely normal for every Iron Condor — including all of Paul's positions, e.g. SPY: Max Loss $647 vs Max Return $353 (1.8×); XLE at target price: Max Loss ~$135 vs Max Return ~$65 (2.1×).但只要价格算对（接近中间价），最大亏损大于最大盈利对任何铁鹰期权来说都完全正常——包括Paul目前所有持仓，例如SPY：最大亏损$647 vs 最大盈利$353（1.8倍）；XLE以目标价成交：最大亏损约$135 vs 最大盈利约$65（2.1倍）。

🧮 Q: What does the "Net Δ" number mean?问：「净Delta」这个数字是什么意思？▸

  • Net Δ measures how much your position's value moves when the stock price moves $1. NVDA: +0.04 means if NVDA rises $1, the position gains about $0.04 (roughly $4 per $100 stock move, for 1 contract).净Delta衡量股价每变动$1，你的持仓价值会跟着变动多少。NVDA：+0.04代表NVDA若上涨$1，持仓约赚$0.04（1张合约下，股价每涨$100约赚$4）。
  • It's close to zero on purpose — an Iron Condor is built to be "delta-neutral": you don't need the stock to go up or down, you just need it to stay between your two short strikes. Near-zero = balanced, working as designed.数字接近零是刻意设计的——铁鹰策略追求「delta中性」：不需要股价上涨或下跌，只需要它停留在两个卖出行使价之间即可。接近零代表持仓平衡，运作正常。
  • If it drifts toward ±0.30–0.50, the stock has moved close to one of your short strikes — that side is starting to act like owning (or shorting) the stock outright, and it's worth a closer look at the Cushion/Variance numbers on the Positions tab.若数字往±0.30–0.50靠近，代表股价已接近某一侧的卖出行使价——该侧开始表现得像直接持有（或做空）股票，此时该多留意「持仓」页签上的缓冲/差幅数字。

💡 Why Paul trades the Iron Condor (IG): sell four option legs (short put + long put + short call + long call) that profit when a stock stays inside a range — the win comes from time decay (theta), not from guessing whether the stock goes up or down. 💡 Paul为何交易铁鹰（IG）策略：卖出四条期权腿（卖出认沽+买入认沽+卖出认购+买入认购），只要股价维持在区间内就能获利——获利来自时间价值衰减（theta），而非猜测股价涨跌方向。

✅ Core Benefits✅ 核心优势

  • Defined, capped risk — the long put and long call ("wings") cap the maximum possible loss the moment you enter, unlike selling a naked put/call. You always know Max Loss before the trade, e.g. the SPY IC's Max Loss is a known ~$647 (per the 2026-07-21 log entry).风险有上限 ——买入的认沽与认购（「翼」）在建仓当下就锁定了最大可能亏损，不像卖出裸期权那样风险无上限。建仓前就能知道最大亏损，例如SPY铁鹰的最大亏损已知约$647（见2026-07-21日志）。
  • High probability of profit — the short strikes are set wide of the current price, so the stock has room to move in either direction and the trade still wins. This is why the realized track record below shows a high win rate.获利概率高 ——卖出行使价设在离现价较远的位置，股价往任一方向波动都还有空间，交易仍可获利。这也是下方已实现战绩显示高胜率的原因。
  • Market-neutral (delta-near-zero) — a well-centred condor doesn't need the stock to go up or down, just to stay roughly where it is. See the Net Δ figures on the Premium tab, all close to zero by design.市场中性（Delta接近零） ——一个居中良好的铁鹰不需要股价上涨或下跌，只需维持在原地附近。见「权利金」页签的净Delta数字，设计上皆接近零。
  • Theta works for you, not against you — every day that passes without a big move, you keep more of the collected premium. Unlike owning stock, time passing is a tailwind, not a cost.Theta站在你这边 ——只要没有大幅波动，每过一天就多保留一些已收权利金。与持有股票不同，时间流逝是助力而非成本。
  • Capital-efficient — margin required is roughly the width of the wings (e.g. $10 wide = ~$1,000/contract before credit), far less capital than buying 100 shares outright, for a defined-risk income trade.资金效率高 ——所需保证金大致等于翼宽（例如$10宽 = 每份合约约$1,000，扣除权利金前），远低于直接买入100股所需资金，却能做到风险有限的收息交易。
  • Flexible / adjustable — positions can be closed early at a target (e.g. buy back at 50% of credit, per the SPY IC plan), rolled, or adjusted before the 15-DTE exit rule (T31) forces a decision.灵活可调整 ——可提前在目标价平仓（例如以权利金50%买回，如SPY铁鹰计划），也可展期或调整，不必等到DTE15离场法则（T31）强制做决定。

⚠️ Trade-offs to keep in mind⚠️ 需留意的取舍

  • Max profit is capped at the credit received — unlike owning the stock outright, there's no upside beyond that even if the stock rallies hard.最大获利上限为所收的权利金——不像直接持有股票，即使股价大涨，收益也不会超过这个上限。
  • Still needs monitoring — the Cushion tab's Variance check and the 15-DTE rule exist because a big enough move can still breach a short strike.仍需持续留意——「缓冲」页签的差幅检查与DTE15法则的存在，正是因为足够大的波动仍可能击穿卖出行使价。
  • 4 legs = 4x the commissions of a single-leg trade, both on entry and on close.4条腿=单腿交易手续费的4倍，建仓与平仓时都是如此。
  • Earnings-date gap risk — a stock can jump straight through both strikes overnight on an earnings surprise, which is why MSFT's IC was flagged but not entered ahead of its 29 Jul 2026 earnings (see IG Cushion Watch log).财报日跳空风险——股票可能因财报意外一夜之间直接跳空穿越两个行使价，这也是MSFT铁鹰在2026年7月29日财报前被标记但未建仓的原因（见IG缓冲监测日志）。
