S36: A New Way Up — Another Summit at Bukit Timah

S36：新的登顶之路——再次登顶武吉知马

Every day, near my house, there is a forest waiting for me. Today it showed me a corner I had never seen before — and it led to a summit again.

每天，在我家附近，都有一片森林在等着我。今天它让我看到了一个从未见过的角落——而这条路，再一次通向山顶。

The Same Forest, A Different Door同一片森林，不同的入口

Hiking near my house has become a daily habit since I retired. Bukit Timah Nature Reserve is only minutes from home, and I have walked into it from more entrances than I can count — the Dairy Farm side, the quarry side, the BKE side. Back in June I climbed to the summit itself for the first time, up the Dairy Farm Trail, one careful step at a time. I thought I had more or less mapped the place out in my head.

退休之后，在家附近徒步已经成了我每天的习惯。武吉知马自然保护区离家只有几分钟路程，我从数不清的入口走进去过——乳农园那一侧、采石场那一侧、还有靠近武吉知马快速公路的那一侧。今年六月，我第一次沿着乳农园步道，一步一步小心地登上了山顶。我以为自己心里大概已经把这地方摸清楚了。

Today I was wrong, in the best way. I set off this morning on the Jungle Fall Path — a route I had never taken before — aiming, again, for the summit. New route, same destination. That is one of the quiet pleasures of hiking somewhere so close to home: you can keep discovering it, over and over, without ever leaving your own neighbourhood.

今天，我错了——而且是以最好的方式错了。今天早上，我走上了从未走过的丛林瀑布步道（Jungle Fall Path），目标依旧是——再一次——山顶。路线是新的，目的地却是老朋友。这正是在离家这么近的地方徒步的一种安静的乐趣：你可以一次又一次地重新发现它，而完全不必离开自己的社区。

A Quiet Surprise Along the Way路途中的一个静谧惊喜

Not far up the Jungle Fall Path, I came across something I did not expect: a small, hand-built resting point tucked into the forest, half-hidden under ferns and tall secondary growth. A rough wooden boardwalk crossed a trickle of water, and beside it stood a little stone-and-wood spout, water running steadily down over moss-covered rocks into a shallow basin below. A knotted rope served as a handrail along the crossing. A weathered wooden bench sat just past it, waiting for anyone who wanted to stop and listen to the water for a minute.

沿着丛林瀑布步道走不远，我遇到了一样意想不到的东西：森林深处藏着一个小小的、手工搭建的休息点，半掩在蕨类植物和高大的次生林之下。一条粗糙的木质栈道横跨过一道细细的水流，旁边立着一个小小的石木引水口，水沿着长满青苔的石头稳稳地流下，注入下方一个浅浅的水池。一条打了结的绳子沿着这段栈道充当扶手。再往前，一张风化的木长凳静静地立着，等着任何想停下来听一听流水声的人。

I stopped there for a photo — cap on, backpack still on my shoulders, the light coming through the canopy in soft green patches. It is easy to walk past small things like this when you are focused on reaching a summit. I am glad I did not, this time.

我在那里停下来拍了张照片——戴着帽子，背包还背在肩上，光线透过树冠洒下柔和的绿色光斑。当你一心只想着登顶时，很容易就会错过这样的小细节。这一次，我很庆幸自己没有错过。

Jungle Fall Hut — Checking the Map, Reading the Signs丛林瀑布小屋——查看地图，阅读告示牌

The path opened up at Jungle Fall Hut, a shelter with a big laminated park map bolted to its noticeboard. I stood there with my finger on the "You Are Here" marker, tracing the red line of the Jungle Fall Path back to where I had come from, and forward along Rengas Path toward the Summit. It is a small habit of mine — I like to actually stop and confirm a route rather than just trust my legs. Right beside the map was another board, this one about the reserve's stream habitats: 16 native fish species and 8 decapod species recorded in these waters, several of them endangered, with a clear reminder not to enter the streams. A few other hikers passed through while I read it — the hut is clearly a popular junction point.

步道在丛林瀑布小屋（Jungle Fall Hut）处豁然开朗，这是一座有盖亭子，告示板上装着一张大幅的护贝公园地图。我站在那里，手指按在"您在此处"（You Are Here）的标记上，沿着红色路线往回追溯丛林瀑布步道的来处，再往前追踪楹树步道（Rengas Path）通往山顶的方向。这是我的一个小习惯——比起单凭双腿去走，我更喜欢真正停下来确认路线。地图旁边还有另一块告示牌，介绍保护区的溪流生态：这里的水域记录到16种本地鱼类和8种十足目物种，其中好几种是濒危物种，牌子上也清楚提醒游客不要进入溪流。我在阅读的时候，有几位其他的徒步者也经过这里——这座小屋显然是一个人气很旺的交汇点。

Not far from the hut, tucked into a wall of leaves and half-swallowed by the forest, I found a faded warning sign: CLIFF EDGE DANGER — DO NOT GO BEYOND THIS POINT. The paint has weathered to almost nothing, and lichen has spread across the lettering. Whatever edge it once guarded is now thick with vegetation, but the sign is still doing its job — I did not go beyond it.

在小屋不远处，藏在一片叶墙之中，几乎被森林吞没，我发现了一块褪色的警告牌：悬崖边缘危险——请勿越过此点。油漆已经风化得几乎看不清，字迹上也长满了地衣。它当年守护的那道边缘，如今已被茂密的植被覆盖，但这块牌子依然尽职尽责——我没有越过它。

"I keep finding new corners of a forest I thought I already knew. Maybe that is the real lesson of hiking near home — the summit is the same, but the way you arrive there never has to be."

"我不断在这片自以为已经熟悉的森林里，发现新的角落。也许这就是在家附近徒步真正的意义所在——山顶始终一样，但抵达它的方式，永远可以不同。"

Up Rengas Path to the Summit沿楹树步道登顶

From the hut, a wooden signpost pointed the way onward: "Rengas Path / Summit," with an arrow into a fresh flight of concrete steps, rope handrails on both sides. This was the last stretch — the point where the walk stops being a wander and starts being a climb again. I have gone up plenty of staircases like this one, but never from this direction. Every step still felt new.

从小屋出发，一块木质路牌指向前方："楹树步道／山顶"，箭头指向一段崭新的混凝土台阶，两侧都有绳索扶手。这是最后一段路——从悠闲漫步转为再次攀登的转折点。这样的台阶我走过不少，但从来没有从这个方向走过。每一步依然感觉是新的。

Still Climbing at 70七十岁，依然在攀登

I am 70 years old, retired for three years now. Adventure sports and travel are still very much part of who I am, and this little forest near my house lets me feed that part of myself almost every single day, without a flight or a visa or a packed bag. A new path — Jungle Fall to Rengas, a hut, a map, a weathered danger sign, and a wooden crossing I had never seen before — and one more summit reached. That was more than enough for one Saturday morning.

我七十岁了，退休已经三年。冒险运动和旅行依然是我生命中重要的一部分，而家附近的这片小小森林，几乎每天都能让我满足这份心愿——不需要搭飞机，不需要签证，也不需要收拾行李箱。一条新路线——从丛林瀑布步道到楹树步道，一座小屋，一张地图，一块风化的警告牌，还有一处从未见过的小小木栈道——再加上再一次登顶。对一个周六的早晨来说，这已经足够美好。
