T56: The Close-Target Rule — 50% of Credit or 21 DTET56：平仓目标法则——50%权利金或21DTE

Close at 50% of credit collected, OR 21 DTE — whichever comes first达到50%已收权利金，或剩21天到期——以先到者为准
Applies to 4-leg Iron Condors AND single-leg short puts, with one nuance below适用于4腿铁鹰与单腿卖出认沽，唯有一点需留意（见下文）

The Two Triggers两个触发条件

Trigger 1 — Profit Target条件1——获利目标Unrealized/realized P&L reaches 50% of the entry credit collected. Lock in the win rather than let theta squeeze out the last dollars while risk sits open.未实现/已实现盈亏达到建仓权利金的50%。锁定获利，而不是为了榨出最后几块钱继续持有风险敞口。
Trigger 2 — Time Target条件2——时间目标Position reaches 21 days to expiry (DTE), regardless of P&L level. Close, roll, or reassess — don't just let it ride.持仓剩21天到期（DTE），无论当前盈亏如何。此时应平仓、展期或重新评估——不要放任其继续持有。
Rule of thumb经验法则Whichever trigger hits first wins哪个条件先触发，就以哪个为准

Why 21 Days Specifically?为何偏偏是21天？

Gamma — how fast the option's delta (and therefore P&L) swings per $1 move in the underlying — accelerates hardest in the final ~3 weeks before expiry. Theta decay (time value melting away) is also fastest in this window, which sounds good for a seller, but it comes bundled with much bigger P&L swings on any adverse move in price. Past 21 DTE, the risk-per-day taken on to capture the remaining premium stops being a good trade — you're holding more gamma risk for less theta reward than earlier in the trade's life.Gamma——即标的每变动$1，期权delta（进而盈亏）摆动的速度——在到期前最后约3周内加速最快。此时Theta（时间价值流失）衰减也最快，对卖方看似有利，但同时伴随着股价一旦出现不利变动时更剧烈的盈亏波动。过了21DTE后，为了榨取剩余权利金而承担的「每日风险」已不再划算——你承担的Gamma风险，相对能拿到的Theta回报，已经不如交易早期那么值得。

This is the same reasoning tastytrade's own historical studies popularized — 21 DTE isn't a magic number, it's just where the risk/reward curve for holding vs. closing tends to flip for a short-premium position.这与tastytrade自家历史研究所推广的理念相同——21DTE并非什么神奇数字，只是卖方权利金部位「续持」与「平仓」的风险/回报曲线，通常在此处发生翻转。

Does This Apply to a Single-Leg Short Put Too?这条规则也适用于单腿卖出认沽吗？

Yes, 21 DTE is still a reasonable default — the gamma/theta acceleration is the same physics regardless of how many legs are in the trade. But two things shift the calculus vs. a 4-leg Iron Condor:是的，21DTE仍是合理的默认门槛——无论交易有几条腿，Gamma／Theta的加速原理都相同。但相对于4腿铁鹰，有两点会改变判断：

1
OK with assignment? DTE stops being a forced deadline若愿意被指派（接股）？DTE就不再是强制期限
If you're happy to own the shares at the strike (the "wheel" mindset — e.g. bullish on GOOGL at $305), 21 DTE isn't a must-close trigger. You can let it ride to expiry or get assigned; the clock only matters if you specifically don't want the stock.若你愿意以行权价接下股票（「轮动策略」思路——例如看好GOOGL在$305接股），21DTE就不是强制平仓的触发点。可以放到到期或被指派；只有在你明确不想接股时，时间才真正重要。
2
Not looking for assignment? Risk is actually bigger per contract不想被指派？每口合约的风险其实更大
A single short put has no long put underneath capping the downside — unlike a condor's wing, which defines max loss. So if the goal is pure premium (not stock), a tested strike near 21 DTE deserves the SAME urgency as an Iron Condor's cut-loss check — arguably more, since there's more capital at risk per contract with nothing hedging it.单腿卖出认沽没有下方买入认沽腿来封顶亏损——不像铁鹰的翼腿，能界定最大亏损。因此，若目标纯粹是赚取权利金（而非接股），行权价一旦在21DTE附近被测试，就应给予与铁鹰止损检查同等甚至更高的重视程度——因为每口合约承担的风险更大，且完全没有对冲。
