📸 Recommended Photo Stop📸 推荐拍照点
B. Ueerhe World Devil City (乌尔禾世界魔鬼城)B. 乌尔禾世界魔鬼城
A yardang wind-city of wind-carved towers and ridges — timed for golden-hour light, when the eroded formations throw the longest shadows and the whole "ghost city" nickname makes sense. One of the trip's most photogenic stops. 风蚀雕琢出的雅丹地貌"风城"——建议在黄金时刻前往，此时侵蚀地貌的阴影最长，"魔鬼城"之名也最为贴切。此行最具拍照价值的地点之一。
🍗
🍽️ Recommended Restaurant Stop🍽️ 推荐餐厅点
C. Karamay Big Plate Chicken (克拉玛依大盘鸡)C. 克拉玛依大盘鸡
Xinjiang's iconic dapanji — chicken braised with potatoes, peppers and wide belt noodles, shared family-style — paired with Wusu beer. A hearty arrival dinner in Karamay after a long day on the road and at the Devil City. 新疆招牌大盘鸡——鸡肉与土豆、辣椒、皮带面同炖，一大盘分食——配乌苏啤酒。经过一整天的长途驾驶与魔鬼城游览后，在克拉玛依的丰盛抵达晚餐。
