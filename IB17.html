Why "Below the Strike" Still Shows a Loss为什么「低于行权价」仍显示亏损

A covered call's mark isn't a simple "did price breach the strike" test either — the option itself has its own market price, driven by how close the stock is to the strike and how much time is left, not just whether it's technically in- or out-of-the-money. FDS rallied from ~$258 toward $272.52 since the call was sold — still comfortably below $300, but that move alone was enough to push the call's own price from the $2.10 it was sold for up to $3.67. As the option seller, a pricier option is a bigger liability against Paul, so the position marks negative even though FDS never got close to $300. 备兑看涨期权的市值也并非简单的「股价是否突破行权价」测试——期权本身有自己的市场价格，由股价与行权价的距离及剩余时间决定，而非仅仅取决于是否技术性价内/价外。自卖出该期权以来，FDS股价从约$258上涨至$272.52——仍明显低于$300，但这一涨幅已足以将该看涨期权的价格从卖出时的$2.10推高至$3.67。作为期权卖方，期权价格上涨意味着Paul的负债增加，因此即使FDS从未接近$300，仓位仍会显示浮亏。
