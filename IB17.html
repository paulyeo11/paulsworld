Why "Below the Strike" Still Shows a Loss为什么「低于行权价」仍显示亏损

A covered call's mark isn't a simple "did price breach the strike" test either — the option itself has its own market price, driven by how close the stock is to the strike and how much time is left, not just whether it's technically in- or out-of-the-money. Between the 4 Aug and 5 Aug snapshots the call's own price kept climbing, from the $2.10 it was sold for up to roughly $4.52 (per Tiger's Market Value ÷ 100). As the option seller, a pricier option is a bigger liability against Paul, so the position keeps marking more negative even without a confirmed fresh FDS share price in this snapshot. 备兑看涨期权的市值也并非简单的「股价是否突破行权价」测试——期权本身有自己的市场价格，由股价与行权价的距离及剩余时间决定，而非仅仅取决于是否技术性价内/价外。从8月4日到8月5日的快照之间，该期权自身价格持续攀升，从卖出时的$2.10涨至约$4.52（按Tiger市值÷100计算）。作为期权卖方，期权价格上涨意味着Paul的负债增加，因此即使本次快照未重新核对FDS股价，仓位仍持续走向更深的浮亏。
