Why the Cost to Close Rose — AVGO Rallied Toward Its Short Call为什么平仓成本上升——AVGO股价上涨逼近卖出认购行权价

The 17 Jul condor sold the 350 put and the 430 call (bought 340P/440C for $10 wings both sides), expiring 28 Aug'26. Cost to close isn't a "did price cross the strike" test — it's what it costs today to buy back all 4 legs, and an option's price is driven mainly by how close the stock is to that strike, not just whether it's technically in- or out-of-the-money. This is the exact same mechanic that cost SPY −$148.60 on 4 Aug: proximity alone inflates the short leg's buy-back price. 7月17日建仓的铁鹰卖出了350认沽与430认购（买入340P/440C，两侧翼宽均为$10），2026年8月28日到期。平仓成本并非「股价是否突破行权价」的测试——而是今天买回全部4条腿需要花多少钱，而期权价格主要取决于股价与该行权价的距离，并非仅仅是技术性的价内/价外。这与8月4日让SPY亏损−$148.60的机制完全相同：仅仅是「逼近」就足以推高卖出腿的买回价格。
